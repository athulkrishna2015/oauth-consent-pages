Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This privacy policy describes how My App ("the app") handles information. The app is a personal, single-user application and is designed to collect as little data as possible.

Information we collect

The app may request limited access to your Google account (for example, your email address or basic profile information) solely to sign you in and provide the app's core functionality. We do not sell your personal information.

How we use information

Any information accessed is used only within the app to deliver its features to the authorized user. It is not shared with third parties except as required by law.

Data retention and security

Access tokens and account information are stored only as needed for the app to function and are protected using standard security practices. You may revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Your choices

You can choose not to grant requested permissions; in that case, the related features will not be available. You can revoke previously granted access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact the app owner using the contact information provided with the app listing.
